01 / When to bring Jason in

When Shopify is the part slowing things down.

  • The campaign is ready, but the landing page is not.
  • A client asks for Shopify work your team does not usually take on.
  • Your team is already full, but the launch still has to happen.
  • A PDP or landing page is holding up media, email or SMS.
  • You are hiring or staffing up, but this project cannot wait.
  • A good client could grow if the Shopify work had a clear owner.

02 / Why teams bring Jason in

Keep the project moving without building a new department.

  • Keep client momentum without hiring.
  • Protect campaign performance after the click.
  • Add Shopify capacity without building a new department.
  • Give one person clear ownership of the store work.
  • Expand project scope without expanding internal overhead.

04 / Agency protection

Your client stays your client.

  • You keep the client relationship and the context.
  • The delivery model is agreed before work starts.
  • Jason does not go around you to sell more work.
  • White-label and confidential work stays confidential.
  • Public proof only happens with written approval.
  • Scope, revisions, communication and ownership are written down.
  • If the project is not a fit, you hear that before money is wasted.
