14 / FAQ

Before you apply

What is included in a Growth Sprint?

Every sprint begins with diagnosis. The final scope may include Shopify design, product pages, merchandising, offers, landing pages, campaign creative, email, SMS, analytics or one controlled advertising test. Only the capabilities required for the agreed priority are included.

Does every sprint include a complete redesign, ads and email?

No. More deliverables do not automatically create a better result. The scope is built around the primary objective and agreed before work begins.

How much does it cost?

Growth Sprint engagements start at $3,000. The final investment depends on the scope, number of pages, catalogue, assets, integrations and campaign requirements.

Is this only for large brands?

No. The studio is specifically designed for growing founder-led brands that have outgrown DIY but are not yet ready to hire an entire e-commerce team.

Do you guarantee sales?

No. Sales depend on product demand, pricing, traffic, inventory, fulfillment and other conditions outside the studio's control. We guarantee the agreed work, process and level of care, not a specific revenue result.

Can we continue afterward?

Yes. Qualified clients may continue through a separate monthly support engagement when there is a genuine recurring priority.

Can I hire you only for creative or branding?

Yes. Smaller standalone engagements are available when a complete Growth Sprint is unnecessary.

Who will I work with?

You work directly with Jason throughout diagnosis, direction and delivery. Specialists may support defined parts of a project when necessary, but the engagement remains founder-led.
