08 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Why not hire internally?

Hiring internally makes sense when the business is ready to support a full ecommerce team. The sprint builds a stronger foundation first, so future hires inherit clear priorities, systems and documentation.

What if I already have freelancers?

That can work. The sprint gives existing specialists clearer direction, priorities and customer experience context so every piece supports the same system.

What if we need ongoing support?

The sprint starts with the foundation. If there is a clear ongoing priority afterward, we can discuss continued support separately.

Do you guarantee results?

No responsible partner can guarantee sales because demand, traffic, pricing, inventory and fulfillment all matter. The sprint focuses on the parts we can improve directly: the Shopify experience, campaign connection, customer clarity and operating foundation.
