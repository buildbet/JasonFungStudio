12 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Why not hire internally?

Hiring internally makes sense when the business is ready to support a full ecommerce team. The sprint builds a stronger foundation first, so future hires inherit clear priorities, systems and documentation.

Why don't you guarantee sales?

Sales depend on demand, traffic, inventory, pricing, offer strength and fulfillment. The sprint focuses on the parts we can responsibly improve: the customer journey, Shopify experience, campaign connection and operating foundation.

What if I already have a designer?

That can work. The sprint can focus on direction, diagnosis, structure and implementation support so existing specialists are working from the same customer experience strategy.

Can we continue afterward?

Yes. If there is a clear ongoing priority after the sprint, we can discuss continued support separately.

Can I keep my current Shopify theme?

Often, yes. The sprint is not automatically a redesign. We decide what changes based on the biggest constraint and what your current theme can support.

How do you decide what gets worked on?

Every sprint starts with diagnosis. We identify the most important constraint, prioritize the work that can realistically improve it, and document what should happen next.
