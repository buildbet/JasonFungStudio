17 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Why not hire internally?

You may eventually need to. The problem is not hiring a team. The problem is hiring people before the customer journey, roles and priorities are clear. The Sprint creates a stronger foundation for future hires to inherit.

What if I already have freelancers?

They can remain involved. The Growth Sprint can create a clearer strategy and shared customer journey for their work. The objective is not necessarily to replace good partners. It is to prevent every person from working toward a different outcome.

What if I only need a website redesign?

A visual redesign may be part of the Sprint, but design decisions are made around the customer journey and business priorities. If the diagnosis shows that you only need a focused redesign, the scope can remain focused.

Do you manage paid advertising?

Paid campaign strategy, creative direction, setup or launch support may be included when relevant to the Sprint. Ongoing media buying is not automatically included. The exact responsibility is defined before work begins.

What if I need ongoing support?

Ongoing support may be available after the Sprint. However, the initial goal is to leave you with a system, documentation and next-step roadmap you can understand and own.

Can everything be completed in 30 days?

The prioritized Sprint scope can be completed within the agreed 30-day engagement when access, content and approvals are provided on time. The purpose is to identify and complete the highest-leverage work first.

How much time will I need to contribute?

Most founder involvement happens during onboarding, strategic decisions and approvals. You will not need to be present every day, but delayed access, content or decisions can affect the timeline.

Do you guarantee results?

No credible studio can guarantee a specific amount of revenue or ROAS. Results are influenced by product demand, pricing, margins, traffic quality, inventory and many other factors. The studio guarantees the agreed scope, direct involvement, clear priorities and documented delivery.

What happens after I apply?

You will answer a short set of questions about your brand, current challenges and next launch or growth objective. If there appears to be a fit, we will schedule a conversation. If the Growth Sprint is not the right next step, I will tell you and recommend a more appropriate direction where possible.
