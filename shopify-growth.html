09 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What exactly do you do?

We help Shopify brands with the areas that directly affect online growth, including the store, campaigns, advertising, offers and customer follow-up. Your exact priorities depend on where your biggest opportunities are.

Does the $500/month plan include everything?

No. The starting plan is designed for brands that need focused ongoing support. We will agree on the priorities and monthly scope before starting. Larger brands or more involved advertising, redesigns and campaign work may require a larger plan.

Do I need to be running ads already?

No. We can help improve your store first, prepare your campaigns, or work with advertising you already have running.

Do you guarantee sales or ROAS?

No. There are too many factors outside any agency's control, including product demand, pricing, inventory and customer behaviour. What we can control is the quality of the strategy, execution, testing and improvement process.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

We can start small. The goal is to prove that working together is useful before making the relationship bigger.

Can you work with my existing team?

Yes. If you already have a designer, media buyer, photographer or another specialist, Jason Fung Studio can work alongside them and help connect the ecommerce execution.

What platforms do you work with?

Our primary ecommerce platform is Shopify.

What happens first?

Book a short call. We will learn about your business, look at what you are currently doing, and determine where we can actually help.
