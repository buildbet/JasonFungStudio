Founder

Why Jason Fung Studio

I started Jason Fung Studio after working at a scaling agency and seeing the gap between what clients actually needed and what large agencies were built to provide.

I understood what clients were asking for, but I also saw how easily brands could feel neglected, overcharged, or pushed through a process that did not give them the results they needed.

So I decided to build my own studio around a different kind of support: direct, founder-led, and focused on doing the work that was actually promised.

Instead of treating Shopify, campaigns, creative and follow-ups as separate pieces, I help connect them into one customer journey that brands can understand and improve.

FAQ

Questions before you start.

Do I need a consultation call?

No. The Quick Apply form and Stripe checkout let you purchase without a sales call. If you have a question, message Jason first. He will suggest a call only if it would be useful.

What happens after I pay?

Stripe returns you to an onboarding page where the full onboarding form appears immediately. Jason then reviews your store, marketing, and priorities.

Is ad spend included?

No. Ad spend is paid separately to the advertising platform and is not included in any studio plan.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There is no long-term contract. You can cancel the recurring weekly subscription when you no longer need the support.

Is this a weekly subscription?

Yes. Each plan is a recurring weekly subscription billed by Stripe until cancelled.

What if I do not know what I need?

That is normal. We review the store and current marketing, identify the top priorities, and recommend what deserves attention first.

Can you set up my ads?

Yes. Support can include Meta ad account and campaign setup, tracking review, campaign structure, and initial optimization. Ad spend remains separate.

What if I already tried ads and they did not work?

We review the campaign, creative, offer, audience, tracking, and landing experience to identify likely problems. Sometimes the most useful first step is outside the ad account.

What can you work on?

Shopify improvements, ads, landing pages, offers, email follow-up, campaign creative, and launches. The exact work follows the agreed priorities and plan capacity.

How quickly do you start?

After payment and onboarding, Jason reviews your information and follows up with the first recommended priority. Timing can vary with current capacity and the information provided.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Start with Essentials and move to Growth or Partner when you want more ongoing involvement.

Is this unlimited work?

No. Every plan has limited capacity. Priorities are worked through in order, and larger standalone projects may need separate scope.

Does $125 include every service at the same time?

No. Essentials provides access to the listed areas, but it is designed around one useful priority at a time—not every service simultaneously.

What does brand direction mean?

It means guidance that keeps campaigns, offers, and growth work visually and strategically coherent. It does not include a full logo, identity, or branding package; those are separately scoped.

Do you guarantee sales or ROAS?

No. Product demand, pricing, inventory, competition, and market conditions affect results. We commit to thoughtful prioritization, execution, and improvement—not guaranteed revenue or advertising returns.
